Employee Productivity vs Workload

Problem

Working hours are climbing while productivity falls. Analyse workload, overtime, absenteeism, task completion, engagement and performance to find bottlenecks and recommend workload interventions.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Load timesheets, task tracker, attendance, engagement, performance data
Compute output per hour = tasks completed ÷ hours worked, per team / week
Fit workload–productivity curve (find diminishing/negative-returns point)
Team weekly hours > optimal point ?
YES → flag overload; check absenteeism & engagement trend
Detect bottleneck: team with queue growth > throughput
Simulate hour cap / task rebalancing
Overload list, bottleneck teams, recommended workload caps

Pseudocode

for team in teams:
    team.out_per_hr = tasks_done / hours
curve = fit_quadratic(hours, tasks_done)       # a*h - b*h^2
optimal_h = a / (2*b)
for team in teams:
    team.overloaded = team.avg_hours > optimal_h
    team.bottleneck = team.queue_growth > team.throughput
simulate(cap = optimal_h): tasks = curve(min(h, cap)) ; gain = tasks_after - tasks_before
